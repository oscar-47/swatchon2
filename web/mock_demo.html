Colophon & Consent

FabricFlow uses machine vision to classify fabric structures from photographs. Submissions are processed in-session, anonymised, and never retained beyond your visit. Aggregated image features may be used to improve classification accuracy. By proceeding, you authorise the application to process the images you upload.

This research is funded by the Laboratory for Artificial Intelligence in Design (FabricFlow: AI-Integrated Textile Supply Ecosystem, Project Code: RP2-2), Innovation and Technology Fund, Hong Kong Special Administrative Region.
