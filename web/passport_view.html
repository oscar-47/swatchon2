recyclability pathways

Fibre-to-Fibre Recycling — preferred pathway
Lycra content below 15% allows direct mechanical recycling. Estimated regeneration rate 60–75%. Compliant with EU textile recycling technical requirements.
II.
Chemical Recycling
Low blend content allows cotton fibre separation via hydrolysis. Elastane threads must be removed prior to processing.
III.
Biodegradation
Cotton fibre component is biodegradable under industrial composting conditions. Lycra portion is non-biodegradable; separation recommended before disposal.

Factors to Evaluate
critical considerations
I.
Batch Consistency — Colour & Weight Stability

Cross-batch colour variance is controlled at ΔE ≤ 1.0 (CMC 2:1) and weight deviation within ±3%. This fabric has passed three consecutive batch stability validations. Request a physical standard swatch with GTL lab matching report before placing the first order.

97.2% Batch Consistency
II.
Shrinkage & Post-Wash Dimensional Stability

Pre-washed and pre-shrunk to ≤ 3% in the warp direction. Pattern grading should include standard allowances. Recommended: machine wash under 40°C, low-heat tumble dry or air dry.

Pre-Shrinking Completed High-Heat Dry May Reach 5%
III.
Dyeing Method & Colour Fastness

Supplied as greige; can be reactive-dyed or low liquor-ratio dyed. For deep shades (black, navy, burgundy), confirm wet rubbing fastness meets Grade 3+. Stock colours come with full fastness documentation.

Custom Deep Shades Need Lab-Dip Time
IV.
Certification Document Completeness

Full Transaction Certificate (TC) and third-party test reports (SGS, Intertek) are included, ready for brand-level DFP filing. Significantly reduces compliance audit burden for EU, UK, and US markets.

100% Document Completeness Aligned with EU ESPR
V.
Stretch & Recovery — Lycra Considerations

10% Lycra® provides 30–35% four-way stretch, suited to fitted silhouettes. Adjustable to 15–18% for yoga and compression, or to 5% for relaxed fit and improved recyclability (requires re-sampling).

Elastane Content Adjustable
VI.
Lead Time Planning & Supply Chain Buffer

Build a 1–2 week buffer into product launch timelines. During peak season (September–November), confirm orders at least 60 days in advance. Rush orders by air freight: approximately USD 1.8–2.5 / kg additional cost.

Peak Season · Order 60 Days Ahead
